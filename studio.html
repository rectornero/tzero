Why the name it's a feeling

Art+heartache=Artache

Italians say mal d'amore for the ache of love.

Artache is mal d'arte: the ache you feel for something you love and can't stop making. That's the energy this place runs on.

With AI. By People. the method

AI

AI opens paths.

Images, music, code and text all pass through AI tools, openly. The AI is a compass when we're lost and a sparring partner when we're stuck.

People

We thread the best.

The seed is always human: the idea, the characters, the choices. Out of every path the AI opens, a person picks one and makes it mean something.

IAm: Oltre I Miei Colori.Beyond my own colors. The studio's method has a name.
Against sameness

AI creativity drifts toward a thousand stories with one shape. We push the other way and keep the creator in charge, not a slot machine.

Our one rule the reason behind the name TZERØ

Games that respect your time.

A game should leave you with a story, not a debt. Some games are built to keep you coming back out of obligation, and TZERØ means leaving those behind. Everything Artache makes follows this rule.

  • No choresNo daily logins, streaks or timers that make you play out of duty.
  • No predatory mechanicsNothing designed to squeeze money out of your impulses.
  • Your attention is yoursYou play when you want, for as long as you want, and stop without losing anything.
