The hook

Each boss the crew befriends permanently breaks one gacha mechanic, for every player.

One boss at a time, the crew sets out to break the gacha itself.

Story

Chapter 1

On the first planet, Weary Reach: three explorable pixel maps, dialogue choices that shape how the crew sees you (heart, wonder, resolve), and visual-novel scenes with expressive character busts.

New roguelite mode

“Inside the Machine”

20 to 30 minute runs where your power-ups are broken gacha mechanics. Branching star-chart routes, a mini summon inside each run, optional challenges, and shareable seed and score codes.

Battle

Grid tactics, then Grid-Break

Grid tactics at the core, plus Grid-Break, our signature. When the gauge fills, the grid vanishes and the team fights in real time on a small lit 2.5D stage. The strongest foe is a turn-based showdown with ultimates and cut-ins. Auto-play everywhere.

Crew

All nine crew playable

Each with skills, an ultimate, a passive and a team combo.

Music

Original soundtrack

Made in-house, in a handheld-era tactics-RPG style.

Art

HD splash art

For banners, cards and story scenes; true pixel art in the world.

Ship life

A walkable ship

Crew chats and chores, and bond visits (now with pajama costumes).

Menus

Phone-first menus

Big labelled buttons, an in-game Codex book, a bag with “where to find” links, and a login calendar that never resets on missed days.
