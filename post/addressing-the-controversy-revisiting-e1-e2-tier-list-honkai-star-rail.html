Addressing the Controversy: Revisiting E1-E2 Tier List | Honkai Star Rail

Player safety · warning

An Eidolon tier list ranks how much to buy again

Nero ranked limited and standard 5-star Eidolons, got called out, admitted mistakes and promised a revised list. Eidolons are duplicates, so ranking them is ranking which character to pull again. The real mistake wasn’t the math; it was making dupes look like a normal next step.

  • ranking which dupe to buy
  • math that makes extra copies feel smart
  • a revision that keeps the chase going

Ache’s tip Treat any duplicate as a luxury you never need. One copy plays the whole story.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In today's video, I address the controversy surrounding my first Honkai Star Rail Eidolon tier list, where I ranked limited and standard 5-star character Eidolons based on Prydwen calculations, Quality of Life improvements, and Gameplay Experience improvements. I respond to viewer feedback, acknowledge my mistakes, and present the data and changes for the upcoming revised tier list video. Join me as I dive into your comments and prepare to refine the rankings.
