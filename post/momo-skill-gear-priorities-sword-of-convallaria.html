Viewer questions about Momo: rank skills, gear like the Diffusing Prism and Resentment Broom, and her upcoming banners. When viewers ask about banners, you can see where the worry lives: what to save for, what’s next. Tuning gear is fun. Banner forecasts are what get you planning your weeks around the game.
