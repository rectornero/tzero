Recruitment Tips & Roster Building | Pokémon Champions

Player safety · heads up

A 22-hour timer is a reason to come back

Champions’ recruitment, explained: a 22-hour recruit timer, quick coupons, 2,500 VP to keep a Pokémon, daily and weekly missions, limited type-focused tickets. Timers and missions keep you checking in, and ‘plan at least three teams’ triples what you chase. Building ‘regardless of how much you spend’ is nice. Spending nothing is nicer.

  • a 22-hour timer to keep checking
  • limited type-focused tickets
  • ‘plan three teams’ before one

Ache’s tip Let the recruit timer run without you. Check in when you feel like playing, not when the clock says.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

I explain how to build strong teams in Pokémon Champions regardless of how much you spend, focusing on recruitment resources and team roles.

I start by covering the game's recruitment system, including the 22-hour recruit timer, the use of quick coupons and VPs, the 2,500 VP cost to permanently keep and edit a Pokémon, and how trial recruitment works. I also show how daily and weekly missions, tutorials, events, ranked battles, and box-space rewards provide the resources needed to steadily expand your roster. Along the way, I mention the limited type-focused tickets and share the mystery gift code haj1mar1g1ft, which grants 100 recruitment coupons.

From there, I recommend planning at least three different teams instead of investing everything into a single composition. I then explain the main team roles: supporters, including weather/field setters and speed control; DPS or primary carries; sub-DPS; and counter picks.

To finish, I walk through my own Pokémon box and several recruitment choices, using Pokebase and ranked battle data to explain my reasoning and show how I plan future team builds.
