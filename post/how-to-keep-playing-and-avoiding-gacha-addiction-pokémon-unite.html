How to Keep Playing and Avoiding Gacha Addiction | Pokémon Unite

Player safety · proud of you

Look who spotted the trap from the inside

Back in 2024, Nero was already calling out how Pokémon Unite’s timed events push you to spend on skins, and asking if that model fits a family-friendly Nintendo game. He was still in the loop, but he saw it. ...I’m a little proud of him. Don’t tell him. And yes: the matches are fun without buying skins.

  • timed events that rush purchases
  • skins sold with gacha tricks
  • subscriptions that feel ‘cheap’

Ache’s tip Put a skin on a wishlist instead of in your cart. If you’ve forgotten it by the event’s end, it wasn’t love.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In this episode, I discuss the legislative challenges Pokémon Unite faces in Europe, and why its business model isn't suitable for a family-friendly platform like Nintendo. I share tips on avoiding spending too much on in-game skins and highlight how timed events create pressure to spend. Using personal examples, I explain the psychological tricks of gacha elements and why it's important to avoid overspending. I emphasize playing the game for fun without needing to buy skins, while discussing the costs and benefits of the game's currency and subscription models. Join me as I focus on enjoying Pokémon Unite in a healthy, wallet-friendly way!
