7-2 Tournament Player Talks! | Pokémon TCG Pocket

Player safety · heads up

A 7-2 record, and a Crown Rare to envy

Nero interviews Shell, who went 7-2 in a tournament, and they talk about how he pulled a Crown Rare Pikachu and an Immersive Mewtwo EX. Rare pulls inside a winner’s story make luck look like skill. They even discuss ‘the role of luck in the game’. When luck has a role, the shop gets one too.

  • rare pulls shown off as trophies
  • luck mixed into competition
  • comparing your binder to a top player’s

Ache’s tip Admire the good plays, not the lucky pulls. Your deck doesn’t need a Crown Rare to be fun.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In this video, I sit down with Shell, a top player from the recent Pokémon Trading Card Game Pocket Tournament who achieved an impressive 7-2 record. We delve into his tournament experience, the strategy behind his deck selection, and how he managed to pull rare cards like Crown Rare Pikachu and Immersive Mewtwo EX. Shell shares valuable tips for both new and veteran players, discusses deck tech like the Fossil strategy, and talks about the current meta and the role of luck in the game. We also explore the community and organization aspects of online tournaments, comparing them to in-person events. Additionally, we speculate on the future of trades in the game and the rise of fan-made formats. Join us for an engaging conversation whether you're a seasoned player or new to the Pokémon TCG Pocket!
