1.2 FREE S-Rank Selector: Best Pick? | Neverness to Everness

Player safety · warning

A ‘free’ S-Rank, with the shop right behind it

A free S-Rank selector box, and past-Nero helping F2P players pick. Then: base characters cost 720 warp pieces in the shop, about 30 limited pulls. A free pick feels like a gift, and it’s also a reason to keep logging in. He says you don’t need to spend much. Much is a lot more than nothing.

  • a free S-Rank that keeps you logging in
  • 720 warp pieces, about 30 pulls
  • ‘you don’t need to spend much’

Ache’s tip Take the free character, enjoy it, and set your budget at zero before you ever open the shop.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

Inside the Loop: I made this while I was caught in the gacha loop. It stays up as a record of that time, not as advice.

I break down the free S-Rank standard character selection box from Neverness to Everness patch 1.2 and go character-by-character to help low spenders and F2P players choose the right base S-rank for their teams. I cover Daffodill’s hybrid DPS and break-focused utility, Jiuyuan’s Anima sub-DPS and blossom synergy, Hathor’s Lakshana Remora support, Fadia’s strong healing and survivability, Sakiri’s standout attack buffs, defense debuffs, DoT support, and crowd control, and Baicang’s incantation kit and niche team synergies (especially with Daffodill). I also explain how base characters can be bought in the shop for 720 warp pieces (about 30 limited pulls), and why you don’t need to spend much money to enjoy the game.
