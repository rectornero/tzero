The Firefly Drama: A Psychological Standpoint | Honkai: Star Rail

Player safety · heads up

Firefly’s design is great. That’s the problem

Here Nero praises how Hoyoverse builds Firefly with images, voice and little psychological cues, and reads the “last 10 seconds” drama as something beautiful. He’s right that it works. Characters built that carefully are built to make you attached, and attachment is what turns into pulls. You can love a character without paying to hold her.

  • design cues tuned to make you attached
  • drama that keeps her in your head
  • love that turns into “I must pull”

Ache’s tip Love a character through the story and fan art. Feelings are real; they don’t need a receipt.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

Hoyoverse does a great job at describing a character through images, tone of voice, and little psychological cues. The misleading "last 10 seconds" Mina Aoyama talks about in her recent tweet in fact hide something beautiful. Or, at least, this is my take on the subject, as someone who were taught a bit of psychology for work! Let me know your opinion in the comments, and let's talk about the character's design! Mina, sleep well: Firefly is safe, as one of the best designed characters :)
