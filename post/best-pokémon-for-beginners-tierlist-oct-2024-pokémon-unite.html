Best Pokémon For Beginners Tierlist! Oct 2024 | Pokémon Unite

Player safety · heads up

A beginner tier list: start simple, stay calm

Nero sorts Pokémon Unite picks by complexity, from simple ones like Charizard and Gyarados to tricky ones like Mimikyu and Hoopa. Sorting by difficulty is kinder than sorting by power. Still, any tier list can make new players feel they must unlock the “right” Pokémon fast. You don’t.

  • rushing to unlock the “right” picks
  • growth hype that rushes beginners

Ache’s tip Start with one Pokémon you like and learn it slowly. New players don’t owe anyone a meta pick.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In this video, I introduce a tier list focused on the best Pokemon choices for beginners in Pokemon Unite. As the game continues to grow, especially with an expected merger with China, it's crucial to know where to start. I've categorized each Pokemon based on their complexity and gameplay style, highlighting simple choices like Charizard and Gyarados and more complex ones like Mimikyu and Hoopa. Whether you are returning to Pokemon Unite or starting fresh, this guide will help you make informed decisions about your starter Pokemon. I hope this tier guide assists in building a solid foundation for your Pokemon Unite experience. Feel free to ask questions and subscribe for more content.
