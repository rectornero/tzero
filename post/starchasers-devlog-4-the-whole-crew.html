Starchasers devlog #4: the whole crew comes aboard

v0.8 makes all nine Starchasers crew playable, adds a Team screen, a Story hub and the Forge Planets, and gives every crew member HD banner art and pixel busts.

STARCHASERS DEVLOG #4 · V0.8.0 TO V0.8.4 · 5 TO 6 NOVEMBER 2026

Thirty-eight questions, one answer each, and one build: v0.8 is when all nine crew members came aboard as playable characters.

Spoiler warning: this devlog shows how Starchasers plays, from battles to how the gacha breaks. If you'd rather find out in the game, the Starchasers page keeps those parts hidden until you tap.

This is the fourth Starchasers devlog. Devlog #3 ended with a long upgrade list from Nero.

✦ Thirty-eight questions

The studio turned that list into thirty-eight design questions, each with a recommendation, on a shared board Nero answers from: banners, character sheets, the Team, the Forge, maps, bond visits, the in-game Codex, the Bag. Building started after his answers, so every piece of v0.8 is one of his decisions.

✦ v0.8.0: the whole crew

v0.8.0 shipped all of it as one build, for Android and Windows: a new Home and a Story screen, all nine crew playable with a Team screen and auto-play, Forge Planets where you earn gear and the crew's training material, crew visits, and smoother walking on the maps.

✦ v0.8.1 to v0.8.2: art for everyone

Then came the art. Every crew member got an HD summon banner, and Nero set the rule for them: cosy, candid moments at home on the ship, warm and kind, never menacing. The level-up screens got full-screen art of the crew out adventuring on planets. Crew cards got pixel portraits, the newest crew fight as pixel chibis, and gear and currencies got real painted icons.

At home on the ship: four of the nine summon banners.

✦ v0.8.3 to v0.8.4: busts and a 2.5D stage

The last two builds were tests that won. Big waist-up pixel busts with four expressions each became the default look of the character sheet (Nero's verdict: "much better!"). And Grid-Break moved onto a small lit 3D stage, with the pixel sprites inside it and a camera that swoops around the fight.

✦ Also in these builds

  • Story hub: one screen for the main story, crew stories and events, so you always know what to play next.

  • Forge Planets: gear tiers, painted backdrops and a vault, and clearing early as the challenge.

  • Pixel crew cards and painted icons for gear, Signatures and Cheat Sheets.

  • Bond poses for visits, and smoother walking on the maps.

  • Sharper art: banners, level-up sheets and cards re-cut at 960 px for phones.

✦ What's next

v0.8.4 is the build on the main line today. The next one is bigger than everything so far, and it's the one Nero will test as a whole: devlog #5.
