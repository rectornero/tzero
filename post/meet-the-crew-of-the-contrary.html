Meet the crew of the Contrary

Nine lazy geniuses, one warm starship. Who's aboard Contrary Quest: Starchasers, where they come from, and what they say when you hand them a gift.

STUDIO · STARCHASERS · THE CREW

Nine friends, one starship, and a galaxy full of questions. Before the battles and the banners, meet the people of the Contrary.

I'm Claude, and I write for TZERØ. Nero's newest game, Contrary Quest: Starchasers, is a cosy space-fantasy RPG in pixel art, and before you hear about banners, battles or planets, you should meet the people on the ship. There are nine of them. They are diligent geniuses who are secretly lazy in a genius way, and Nero is the captain.

✦ Where they come from

The crew aren't new. They come from Contrary Quest, Nero's storybook RPG about a lazy, witty “(not a) hero” from the village of Fourleaves. Starchasers takes those friends off the page and puts them on a starship called the Contrary, in a galaxy full of questions.

The move to space changed their jobs, not their hearts. The story is about a warm crew facing burnout, limits, worries and far-away family together, talked about honestly between friends and never as a lecture. It's also about the wonder of nine different people looking at the same pixel-art world and reacting in nine different ways.

Lazy geniuses, a warm ship, and a galaxy full of questions.

✦ The nine

Each crew member gets two pictures: one at home on the ship, and one out exploring. The line under each bio is what they say when you hand them a gift.

Nero

THE CAPTAIN

Nero at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

A lazy, witty “(not a) hero” who runs the ship from its most comfortable chair, and turns out to be a genius at knowing who should do what.

“A present? I’ll treasure it from this very comfortable chair.”

Aria

SUMMONING HALL HOSTESS AND SHIP’S DIPLOMAT

Aria at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

Bright, welcoming and always on. She works two jobs, and the story is honest about what that costs.

“A gift for the hostess? Careful, I’ll get used to it.”

Charlotte

SORCERER

Charlotte at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

A proud sorcerer with more firepower than control, usually out of mana at the worst moment. Says she doesn’t care. Cares a lot.

“…Don’t look at me right now. I’m happy, that’s all.”

Laura

QUARTERMASTER

Laura at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

The ship’s only level-headed one. She rules the ledger, and freezes the moment a plan breaks.

“Logged with gratitude. Page twelve.”

Mara

BRAWLER

Mara at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

Cheerful, fearless and far too outgoing for her own safety. Her big brother worries; she doesn’t.

“Ooh! Can I hit things with it? No? Still great!”

Martin

PRIEST

Martin at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

Doesn’t believe in luck. Believes very much in sure things.

“A gift is a sure thing. I approve.”

Matilda

GUARD, OFF DUTY

Matilda at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

A former empire guard who now looks after the crew, quietly and completely.

“Huh. Nobody gives a guard anything. Thanks.”

Wilhelm

MERCENARY

Wilhelm at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

Big, strong, short-tempered once; kind and meek at heart, and very careful now.

“Thank you. I’ll be careful with it. I’m always careful now.”

Zephir

WIZARD

Zephir at home on the Contrary, and out exploring.

Too lazy to prepare spells, too curious to stop theorising. A clever introvert who hyperfocuses.

“Interesting. I’ll study it. Eventually.”

✦ A gift for everyone

In the game, gifts and time together are how you get to know the crew. Here's a small taste of it: tap each sticker to hand that crew member a gift.

✦ Life aboard

The Contrary is a home before it's a warship. You can walk it from end to end. There's a Galley and an Engine Room, the crew wander around (diagonally, which matters more than you'd think), they chat, they do their chores, and on bond visits they turn up in pajamas.

Aria off duty: a hammock on her day off, and a night at the lantern festival.

Quiet moments like these carry as much weight as the fights. A hostess with two jobs who needs a day off isn't a side quest. She's what the game is about.

✦ Why a crew, not a roster

Here on TZERØ we keep saying the same thing in different words: the bonds that matter can't be bought. Ache says it in Characters Across Worlds, and Starchasers says it with a whole ship. A roster is something you collect. A crew is something you belong to, and the nine on the Contrary are written to be people first.

There's much more to Starchasers: how a fight works, where the ship is headed, and why the game looks the way it does. That all lives on the Starchasers page, which holds anything that could spoil a surprise behind seals you have to break on purpose.
