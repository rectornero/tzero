Ultimate Guide: Characters | Ash Echoes

Player safety · heads up

Seven classes, one tired you

This guide walks through Ash Echoes’ seven classes, elemental reactions like Electro Conduct and Melt, and engraving stats. Big systems feel like mastery, but every new layer is another thing to optimize and another reason to collect more characters to fill every role. You don’t need one of everything.

  • seven classes to fill
  • layers that push you to collect

Ache’s tip Pick a small team you like and learn it well. A full roster is a wish, not a requirement.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In this Ultimate Guide to Ash Echoes, I provide an in-depth explanation of the game's characters and their classes, including Bulwark, Vanguard, Striker, Skirmisher, Ranger, Support, and Tactician. Learn about the unique roles, special abilities, and strategies for each class. Additionally, discover the elemental system and how it influences gameplay through reactions like Electro Conduct, Vaporization, and Melt. Understand the engraving stats, character stats, and how to optimize your team for success. This episode is a must-watch for anyone looking to master Ash Echoes.
