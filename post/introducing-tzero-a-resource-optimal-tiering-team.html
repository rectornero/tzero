Are you a fan of rhythm games like BanG Dream, but struggling to achieve high ranks in tiering events? If so, TZERO might be the solution you've been looking for. TZERO is a tiering team project that emphasizes resource optimization and improving skills, all while encouraging a sense of community among its members.

What is TZERO?

TZERO stands for "T0" (standing outside the first position of a ladder, as we achieve other goals besides tiering) and is also an acronym for: "Team Zero Errors & Resource Optimization". The idea for TZERO came from playing Bandori and realizing the hidden, very high cost of tiering events, combined with the limited availability of in-game resources like stars. TZERO's goal is to provide a solution to these challenges by helping players optimize their resources and improve their skills in rhythm games.

Resource Optimization

One of TZERO's main priorities is resource optimization. By spending the least amount of resources - including money - to achieve the most out of the game, players can achieve high ranks without breaking the bank. TZERO provides support and optimization strategies for gachas and tiering, so that players can make the most of their limited resources. With TZERO's help, players can achieve their goals and still have resources left over for other aspects of the game.

More about Resource Optimization

One of the primary objectives of TZERO is to optimize resources, which includes both time and money spent on the game. We understand that rhythm games can be expensive, and we want to provide support to the players in the community to help them achieve their goals while spending the least amount of resources possible. Our goal is to help players make the most out of their limited stars, in-game currency, and real-life money, without compromising on their enjoyment of the game.

Gacha Optimization: One of the most significant expenses in rhythm games is the gacha

system. Players have to spend in-game currency or real money to get the best cards to compete in events. To optimize gacha spending, TZERO will provide the following strategies:

  • Choose the right time: Gacha rates are sometimes better on certain days of the week or during specific events. TZERO will keep track of the gacha rates and recommend the best time to spend your currency.

  • Prioritize meta cards: Certain cards have a higher statistical value and are essential for tiering. TZERO will recommend players to prioritize these cards when they're available to increase their chances of success.

  • Save stars: We will recommend players to save stars for events where the rewards are valuable. TZERO will also recommend players to spend stars in the most efficient way possible.

Tiering Optimization: Tiering events can be extremely time-consuming, and players often have to spend real money to buy stars to maintain their ranking. To optimize tiering, TZERO will provide the following strategies:

  • Plan ahead: TZERO will help players plan ahead by determining which events they want to tier in and how many resources (in-game currency, stars, and real money) they'll need to spend.

  • Find the most efficient way to tier: TZERO will help players determine the most efficient way to tier. For example, players can use a certain type of card to reduce the amount of time needed to play the event or use specific support members to reduce the number of stars they need to spend.

  • Share tips and tricks: TZERO will encourage players to share their own tips and tricks for tiering events and will also provide a forum for players to discuss their strategies and learn from each other.

Improving Skills

While tiering events require a lot of time and the right cards, skills in rhythm games are just as important. TZERO encourages its members to improve their skills by providing resources for learning charts, understanding patterns, and optimizing board setups. With a focus on skill improvement, TZERO members can achieve even higher ranks in tiering events and have more fun playing the game.

TZERO Hall of Fame

Tiering events can be costly and time-consuming, but TZERO encourages its members to plan their tiering goals and provides support to achieve them. When a member successfully completes a tiering event, their achievement is shared with the group through the TZERO Hall of Fame. This way, every member can celebrate the success of their fellow players and feel a sense of community.
