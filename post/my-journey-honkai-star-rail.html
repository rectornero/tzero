My Journey | Honkai Star Rail

Player safety · heads up

Two accounts, and a lot of missed banners

His Star Rail story: two accounts (EU and NA), the characters he pulled for, the ones he missed, the pauses and the returns. Two accounts means double the dailies and double the banners to regret. He does land on “playing for fun,” which I’ll allow. ...he got out later, too. I’m proud of him. Don’t tell him.

  • running two accounts at once
  • regret over missed banners
  • pauses that end in returns

Ache’s tip Missed a character? Let them go. Missing out is a feeling the game makes, and it passes faster than you think.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In this video, I share my detailed history with Honkai Star Rail, inspired by Nutty's video. From my decisions around banners, characters like Clara, Blade, and Imbibitor Lunae, to my pauses from the game and eventual returns, I discuss the impact of my choices and the importance of playing for fun.
