Overexplained Guide: Momo | Sword of Convallaria

Player safety · heads up

Overexplained, and still not her final form

An ‘overexplained’ Momo guide pieced together from fragmented early info and Taiwan PvP footage, ending with ‘stay tuned’ for her last form. That’s the catch: a unit is never finished. There’s always another form, trinket or PvP build, and studying servers ahead of yours makes you feel behind before you start.

  • a final form that isn’t here yet
  • PvP builds that ask for more
  • other servers showing what’s ‘next’

Ache’s tip Be done with a character when she’s fun, not when she’s complete. ‘Complete’ keeps moving.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

Hello everyone and welcome to the first over-explained guide for Sword of Convallaria. This series will analyze characters in depth, starting with Momo, a tank and sub-DPS character. This guide was requested by BRXYE. Although we have fragmented information due to early global stages, we explore Momo’s key abilities, Doom Waken and Repulsion Barrier, her weapon and trinket recommendations, and strategies for PvM and PvP builds. Special thanks to @iceyfun7410 for sharing Taiwan PvP footage. Stay tuned for more details as we unlock her last form.
