Essential Beginners Guide | Girls' Frontline 2

Player safety · heads up

Rush to level 20 so the timed events find you

Level 20 is the goal here, because that’s where Girls’ Frontline 2’s time-limited events unlock, with Lotta and skins as the prize. Then it explains fortifying characters with duplicates. So lesson one is ‘hurry’ and lesson two is ‘you’ll want copies’. That’s a lot of pressure for week one.

  • a race to unlock timed events
  • dupes needed to fortify units
  • stamina you’re told to spend

Ache’s tip Level at your own pace. If an event ends before you get there, you didn’t lose anything you had.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In today's video, I guide you through the first essential steps in Girls' Frontline 2, focusing on reaching level 20 to unlock time-limited events. I'll show you how to efficiently complete commissions to gain experience, spend stamina on campaigns, and take advantage of supply missions and combat simulations. I'll also walk you through the Time-Limited Events at level 20 where you can grab exciting rewards, including characters like Lotta and various skins and weapons. Additionally, I cover how to fortify characters and optimize your team with duplicates. By following my tips, you'll level up in no time and enjoy the game even more.
