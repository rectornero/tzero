Game Overview | Neverness to Everness

Player safety · heads up

Spending little is good. The loop is still there

Nero plays Neverness to Everness with just the subscription, no battle pass, at his own pace and without rushing stamina. Real progress. But the banners are still here: a 90-pull guarantee, a 200-pull skin, 720 fragments for base characters. Those numbers are long roads built to keep you walking.

  • a subscription that keeps you logging in
  • 200-pull skins and 90-pull guarantees
  • dailies dressed up as mini-games

Ache’s tip Keep your limit, and check in with yourself monthly: is this still fun, or just a routine?

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

I share how I’m enjoying Neverness to Everness while spending very little—just the subscription and no battle pass—by playing at my own pace without FOMO. I show a quick freebie quest near Torchwood Avenue to photograph a paper plane and unlock an apartment anomaly that reduces stamina cost, then explain why I’m not rushing weekly/daily stamina. I break down the gacha and banner economy (90-pull guaranteed limited character, 200-pull skin, 50-pull base selector, and unlocking base characters via the Hunter Exchange with 720 fragments—about 30 pulls’ worth), plus how I’m planning picks like Daffodill and Sakiri for esper cycle synergy. I also cover events, City Tycoon mini-games and ratings, anomaly commissions, story/quests, dailies, and why the battle pass rewards aren’t necessary.
