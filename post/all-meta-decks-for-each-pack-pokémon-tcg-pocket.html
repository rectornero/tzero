All Meta Decks For Each Pack | Pokémon TCG Pocket

Player safety · heads up

Pack economy: card packs are still gacha

For TCG Pocket’s launch, Nero maps the ‘pack economy’: which of the Mewtwo, Charizard or Pikachu packs to open to build the most meta decks. Digital card packs are gacha with a binder. Planning around ‘minimal packs’ is smart, but the plan still starts from the idea that you need every meta deck.

  • packs as random pulls
  • ‘every meta deck’ as the goal
  • launch hype

Ache’s tip Pick one deck you like the look of and play it. You don’t need all of them to have fun.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In celebration of the Pokemon Trading Card Game Pocket's global launch, I'm diving into the best ways to create multiple decks with minimal packs. I'll guide you through the pack economy and show you which packs to prioritize based on deck composition. Utilizing resources from Pokemon Zone, we analyze the strengths of various EX and non-EX decks, including Venusaur EX, Blastoise EX, Charizard EX, and more. I'll also provide tips on which starter pack (Mewtwo, Charizard, or Pikachu) might best suit your play style and needs. Join me, Nero Atlas, as we embark on this exciting journey in the Pokemon Trading Card Game Pocket!
