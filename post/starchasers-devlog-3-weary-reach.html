Starchasers devlog #3: landing on Weary Reach

The Contrary lands on its first planet, Weary Reach: three pixel maps, dialogue choices that shape how the crew sees you, the game's opening, a summer event, and the first Android build.

STARCHASERS DEVLOG #3 · V0.7.0 TO V0.7.25 · 4 TO 5 NOVEMBER 2026

Twenty-five builds in two days. The ship lands, the maps learn to walk cell by cell, and the game gets an opening, a summer, and a phone to live on.

This is the third Starchasers devlog, and the busiest stretch so far. Here's v0.7, from the first landing to v0.7.25.

✦ v0.7.0: the helm and Weary Reach

The Bridge became a place to explore, with spots to find, treasures, crew talks and goals, saved on the device. And the helm finally did something: it lands the ship on Weary Reach, the first planet.

Weary Reach has three maps: the Landing, the Mossway and the Drained Hollow. Fights start when you walk into a nest. And the people you meet ask things of you, with answers that build the crew's alignment in three directions: Heart, Wonder and Resolve. The planet's mini-epilogue follows the way you leaned.

The Bridge, and a choice on the Mossway.

✦ Tiled, cell by cell

Next the maps were rebuilt as tiled 2D maps that walk cell by cell, in the spirit of classic RPG Maker games: first the Landing, then the Mossway and the Drained Hollow, so all of Weary Reach walks the same way. Fights on the map now happen where you are, and show the place's name.

✦ v0.7.14: an opening and a summer

v0.7.14 gave the game its opening: a trailer, the title screen, a playable Prologue and a tutorial on Weary Reach. It also brought the first event, Halcyon Days, a summer on an alien beach, with Aria in summer outfits.

✦ v0.7.16 to v0.7.25: a phone, and full-body pixel art

v0.7.16 was the first Android build. Nero tests every build on his phone since then, so the next ones were about living there: screens that fill a 20:9 phone, bigger text and bigger touch areas.

At the same time the cast came to life in pixel art. Nero asked for large full-body figures, pixel only, and by v0.7.23 the whole cast stood in story scenes that way, with pixel faces in dialogue and in battle, pixel backdrops for the story, a Gallery with a picture viewer, and an Outfit button on the Crew screen.

✦ Also in these builds

  • Goals and treasures: places to find on the Bridge and on Weary Reach, all saved on the device.

  • Locals: pixel NPCs on Weary Reach, each with their own small trouble.

  • Growing the crew: crew XP from exploring and fights, level breakers where you choose a class, and Signature gear forged in battle.

  • The odds board flickers: the first visible crack in the gacha, right on the banner.

  • Gear Bench and Gallery: a bench for gear that fits a phone, and a gallery with a picture viewer.

  • Fixes that matter: a softlock on the Mossway and the Hollow is gone, and every SVG icon now ships in exported builds.

✦ What's next

The upgrade list Nero wrote after v0.7 was long, and it became the biggest single build yet. That's devlog #4.
