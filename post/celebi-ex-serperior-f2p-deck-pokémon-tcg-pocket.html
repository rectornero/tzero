Player safety · heads up

An “F2P deck” is still a deck to open packs for

Nero builds a grass deck around Celebi EX, Serperior and his Caterpie line from the Mythical Island expansion. Calling it F2P is kind, but a new expansion still nudges you to open packs to collect the pieces. Free decks are a nice idea. Just don’t let them turn into a reason to chase cards.

  • a new expansion pulling you back in
  • “F2P” still needing lucky packs

Ache’s tip Build with the cards you already have. If a deck needs luck to finish, it isn’t really free.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)
