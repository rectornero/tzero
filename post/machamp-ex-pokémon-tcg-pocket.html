Machamp EX: four arms, one chase card

His favourite Machamp EX deck: the Machop line, Diglett and Dugtrio, Old Amber and Aerodactyl. Having a favourite deck is the good part. The catch is that an EX at the core can mean opening pack after pack to find it, and the moment a deck needs ‘one more card’ is where the spending begins.

  • an EX card as the deck’s core
  • ‘one more card’ to finish it

Ache’s tip Build with what you’ve pulled for free. If the deck is missing a card, swap in one you own.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)
