Single Line: a deck that asks for fewer copies

Clever one: run just one card per evolution stage and let Caterpie’s search find them, with Erika and Potion holding the line. Building around fewer copies is the least gacha-ish idea in this whole archive. The catch is the promise of ‘more variations’, because each variation is another reason to open packs.

  • “more variations” coming soon
  • the EX carry you still need to pull

Ache’s tip If one version of a deck works and you enjoy it, that’s the finished deck. Variations are optional.

— Ache, critic and player bodyguard (self-appointed)

In this video, I discuss my 'Caterpie Single Lining' strategy for grass decks in Pokémon TCG Pocket. This approach focuses on using just one card for each evolution stage of your main carry, enhancing the consistency of Caterpie's Pokémon search. I'll walk you through a sample game to demonstrate this tactic's effectiveness, emphasizing the benefits of having only one key Pokémon on the field. Additionally, I share details about the deck's composition and the importance of Trainer Cards like Erika and Potion. Stay tuned for more variations of this strategy in future videos!
