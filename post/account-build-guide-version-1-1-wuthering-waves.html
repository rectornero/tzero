“Perfect teams” is a moving target

The promise here is to make perfect teams in Wuthering Waves 1.1 and prepare for endgame. “Perfect” is the trap: endgame keeps moving, and every new version shows off a team that looks more perfect than yours. Nero kept chasing it. Good enough, and fun, is a real place to stop.

  • the “perfect teams” promise
