Liminal Outliers mystery visual novel · in the works

Miriam, 18, lives in a white nine-storey block of flats in a seaside town. Things there feel surreal, uncanny, slightly off. The toy uses a stand-in room, not the story.

Hints

Follow the warm light

No sparkles and no question marks. The only hint in the building is the warm light from the porter's lodge, leaking toward what matters. You learn to read light the way the tenants do.

Move your torch. Find three things the light points at.

ContraryQuest a very short RPG · in the works

A lazy, witty (Not a) hero from the village of Fourleaves, who lives with his grandma Agata. Drawn in storybook ink. The toy uses stand-in villagers, not the story.

Choices

Say no to the quest

Every quest can be turned down, and turning it down is a real choice. The village carries on without you, and the way you dodged it changes what happens next. Sometimes you help by accident.

Three villagers ask for help. Answer like he would.

AI-ICE a two-player card game · said "Alice" · work in progress

AIs try to remember a lost mankind: its dreams, hearts and memories. A match is a fight for discovery, and it ends with both players shaking hands. No dice, no boards.

Card play

Read the dream

Your rival's dream hides a meaning: Music, Fate or Nature. Every card you play makes the dream give away a little hint. Guess it right and its Essence fills faster. Then it fills yours, out of kindness.

Fill their Essence. Watch the hints.

Overgame a social strategy game that lasts weeks · play on Soul Street

Trade favours, sign Contracts, betray, and stay out of the Red Zone. Everyone sees your Zone. No one sees your deals. The toy uses simplified rules, not the real ones.

Practice

A solo night on Soul Street

A full game lasts weeks and needs a whole community. This would be a short solo night against three street regulars, to learn how a deal feels before you play with people. Same rules, nobody real to betray.

Three nights, three deals. Stay out of the red.

DeadenD noir visual novel · Nero's first, 2018

Where the thrillers started. Some memories are better left forgotten. The toy is a made-up case, so nothing here touches the real one.

Choices

Dead ends that teach

A dead end isn't a game over, it's a lesson. Rewind the tape one step and keep what you learned: a code, a name, a door you now know is locked. Failing is how you find the way out.

Get out of the building. You may have to die first.

How the votes work

Hearts are counted on this site, one number per idea. We don't track you: your browser only remembers which ideas you hearted, so the button stays lit. Counts can take a minute to catch up. Hearts aren't a promise. If the most loved idea turns out not to work, we'll say so in the devlog, and why.

Every idea follows our one rule: games that respect your time.
